# Support Center

Welcome to Adverto AI Support.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How does the AI generate creatives?
Our proprietary AI analyzes your target audience, objective, and brand guidelines to generate high-converting ad copy and creatives tailored for Meta and Google.

### 2. Why is my campaign stuck in 'Pending Approval'?
Your creatives need manual approval by default before spending budget. You can turn this off in the Campaign Settings.

### 3. How do I contact billing support?
For billing inquiries, please email `billing@adverto.ai`.
